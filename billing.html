Paid membership

Unless you cancel before the next billing date, a paid membership renews automatically at the price and interval disclosed at checkout. We will not charge you until you have given express consent to the paid subscription terms.

Access normally continues through the period already paid for, unless we suspend it for abuse, fraud, or a legal requirement.
